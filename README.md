# BudgetMate AI — Landing Page

A single-page marketing landing page for **BudgetMate AI**, an AI-powered budgeting and financial forecasting app for young Australians.

Built for SIT726 Task 6.2C (Individual Credit Task) — Deakin University.

**Author:** Yousuf Sinha (s226032987)

## What's in this page

- **Main headline & supporting headline** — leads with the "forecast ahead, not just track behind" angle
- **Hero shot** — a mock ledger/dashboard card showing sample budget, spending and forecast data
- **Benefit summary** — short pitch on why BudgetMate AI is different from typical budgeting apps
- **Call to action** — "Join the waitlist"
- **Feature benefits** — AI Budget Planner, AI Spending Forecast, AI Savings Coach, and automatic account syncing
- **Social proof** — sample testimonials from the target audience (young professionals and students in Australia)

## Design concept

The page is styled like a **checkbook ledger**: ruled lines, tabular numbers, and receipt-style testimonial cards. This ties the visual design directly to the product's subject matter (budgeting) instead of using a generic app-landing-page template.

- **Fonts:** Fraunces (headlines), IBM Plex Mono (numbers/data), Inter (body text)
- **Colors:** paper cream background, dark ledger-green accent, amber used only for forecast alerts
